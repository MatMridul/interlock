# MOTION GRAMMAR: one table for the whole product

Same event, same meaning, same timing family everywhere: stage, tape, scoreboard, chat chips, toasts, drawer, dialogs, autoplay. A component may restyle (the comps do) but may not change the semantics, durations or priorities below. Source of the timings in code: `docs/ui-v2/comps/src/core.js` (`PH`, `Snd.SPEC`, `Hap.PAT`, `Notify`).

**Easing family:** `out = cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` for things arriving, `inout = cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)` for things travelling. Only these two. **Timing family:** micro 120 to 160 ms (press, highlight), standard 300 to 520 ms (arrive, ignite, shudder), travel 620 to 760 ms. Nothing else.

**Global rules.** One primary animation at a time (a FIFO queue; more than 3 waiting plays at 0.55x speed, never dropped). Nothing flashes more than 3 times per second; the largest brightness step in any beat is a single event at least 1.5 s from the next. Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`): no travelling token, no particles, no sweeps; the outcome appears at once with a 160 ms highlight and a 240 ms fade; afterglow is a static mark; tones shrink to a single short tone; haptics shrink to one pulse. Ambient motion pauses when the tab is hidden. Sound and haptics are OFF by default, switched on only by the user's own click (so the audio context is created after a gesture, never at load).

## Notification taxonomy
| Kind | Appears when | Stays | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| info | reset, autoplay, session start | fades after 4.2 s | polite |
| allowed | an ALLOW verdict, a human approval, an executed transfer | fades after 4.2 s | polite |
| blocked | a REJECT verdict, a wrong code, a lock | until the user dismisses it, or a newer blocked toast supersedes it | assertive |
| needs-human | an approval is required and only a human can give it | until the approval happens, is dismissed, or is superseded | assertive |
| error | server unreachable, session lost, malformed response | until retry succeeds or dismissed | assertive |

**Stacking rule:** at most 3 toasts at once. When a fourth arrives, the oldest non-persistent toast is removed first; if all are persistent, the oldest persistent one goes. Every toast has a visible "Dismiss" button reachable by keyboard.

## Sound and haptics: what to expect
- **Sound** is a few synthesized tones (Web Audio oscillators), no files. The toggle is visible in every comp. Nothing plays before the user's first click on the toggle. Under reduced motion each cue becomes one short tone.
- **Haptics** use `navigator.vibrate`. This works on supporting Android browsers (Chrome, Firefox on Android). It does **not** work on iOS Safari or on most desktops, where the call is absent or does nothing. We feature-detect: where unsupported the toggle is simply not shown. Haptics are an enhancement to a screen that already says everything in text and shape.
- Neither channel ever carries information the screen does not also carry.

## Compliance check (every component)
1. Does it use the event names above and no others? 2. Same duration family? 3. Same live-region priority? 4. Same word ("ALLOWED", "BLOCKED", "HUMAN") plus shape plus colour? 5. Does it stay silent when it should (proposed has no sound; state change has none of its own)?
